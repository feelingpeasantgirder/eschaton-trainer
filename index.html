Feature Set

Everything you need to tune your sandbox

Six core modules compiled into one lightweight injector DLL — each toggle is hotkey-bound and works in offline matches only.

🎯

Aimbot

Adjustable FOV, smoothing and bone preference. Snap to targets with a configurable hotkey and per-weapon sensitivity profiles.

👁️

ESP Overlay

See player outlines, health bars, distance readouts and loot markers through terrain, with colour-coded filters you can toggle live.

🧱

Wallhack

Chams-style material override that renders enemies and objectives through solid geometry — ideal for map study and route planning.

🔫

Infinite Ammo

Remove magazine and reserve limits across all weapons, including explosives and special-issue gear, without freezing the reload animation.

🛡️

God Mode Toggle

One-key invulnerability for testing builds and encounters. Damage is nullified client-side and cleanly reverts the moment you toggle it off.

💉

Injector DLL

A signed-free, single-file injector with manual mapping, auto-detach and a clean unload path. No drivers, no services, no leftovers.
